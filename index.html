CS-232 · Dr. Bilal Wajid · Midterm prep

Operating Systems, made easy

Thirteen short, visual lessons covering exactly the midterm syllabus (OSTEP Ch 2, 4–10, 13–16). Each one takes about 15–20 minutes and ends with practice.

The exam Tuesday 6 October, in your normal class slot and room. It's worth 20%, and Dr. Bilal and Dr. Zafar set the paper together. Questions look like the weekly quizzes: trace a schedule, compute averages, trace fork(), explain a memory bug, translate an address.
How to study with this site: active recall Don't just read. After each section there's a recall check: type your answer from memory, reveal the model answer, then mark yourself Got it / Shaky / Missed. Each lesson opens with a short warm-up on earlier lessons. Recall checks also include spot the true one questions: four near-identical options, each wrong one hiding a classic slip, and you get one try. All of them are collected on the drill page. Anything you miss goes to My weak spots.
