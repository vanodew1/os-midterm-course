How to use this
  1. Type your answer before you reveal it, every time. If you only "think" the answer, you'll feel like you know more than you do.
  2. Grade yourself honestly. Mark Got it only if you'd have scored full marks in the exam.
  3. Come back after a gap: tonight, tomorrow morning and Tuesday morning. A card you get right after a break is one you really know.
